== What the documents suggest when a run fails ==
  For each message ADIT finds in the output (the 'possible causes of failure' line of the analysis summary), this quotes what the code's own documents suggest.
  These are not ADIT's recommendations; the numbers and steps are as in the sources, and whether they suit your system is your call.
  - 'convergence NOT achieved after N iterations': the PwBaseWorkChain of aiida-quantumespresso multiplies mixing_beta by 0.8 and restarts from the previous run (the numbers are the values implemented in aiida-quantumespresso: delta_factor_mixing_beta = 0.8); when it happens during a relax it also takes the output structure and restarts from scratch. Source: https://github.com/aiidateam/aiida-quantumespresso/blob/main/src/aiida_quantumespresso/workflows/pw/base.py
  - 'Maximum CPU time exceeded': update to the output structure and continue with restart_mode = 'restart' in CONTROL; the work chain sets max_seconds to 0.95 x the walltime (the numbers are the values implemented in aiida-quantumespresso: delta_factor_max_seconds = 0.95). Source: https://github.com/aiidateam/aiida-quantumespresso/blob/main/src/aiida_quantumespresso/workflows/pw/base.py
  - diagonalization failures ('S matrix not positive definite', 'problems computing cholesky', 'zhegvd failed', 'too many bands are not converged', 'eigenvectors failed to converge', 'Error in routine broyden'): switch diagonalization in ELECTRONS to another of david / paro / cg in turn, and give up once all are tried. Source: https://github.com/aiidateam/aiida-quantumespresso/blob/main/src/aiida_quantumespresso/workflows/pw/base.py
  - 'history already reset at previous step' (BFGS history failure) / 'bfgs failed': for relax set ion_dynamics = 'damp' (for low-symmetry systems first rattle the structure with a random displacement of standard deviation 0.01: the numbers are the values implemented in aiida-quantumespresso rattle_stdev = 0.01); for vc-relax multiply trust_radius_min by 0.1 (the numbers are the values implemented in aiida-quantumespresso delta_factor_trust_radius_min = 0.1), then fall back to ion_dynamics = 'damp' with cell_dynamics = 'damp-w'. Source: https://github.com/aiidateam/aiida-quantumespresso/blob/main/src/aiida_quantumespresso/workflows/pw/base.py
  - the ionic loop does not converge within nstep: update to the output structure and restart from the charge density. Source: https://github.com/aiidateam/aiida-quantumespresso/blob/main/src/aiida_quantumespresso/workflows/pw/base.py
  - 'Not enough space allocated for radial FFT' (large volume contraction in vc-relax): double cell_factor in CELL (default 2 → 4) and restart from scratch. Source: https://github.com/aiidateam/aiida-quantumespresso/blob/main/src/aiida_quantumespresso/workflows/pw/base.py
  - the SCF accuracy gets stuck during a relax: multiply upscale in IONS by 0.3 (minimum 1) and restart (the numbers are the values implemented in aiida-quantumespresso: delta_factor_upscale = 0.3). Source: https://github.com/aiidateam/aiida-quantumespresso/blob/main/src/aiida_quantumespresso/workflows/pw/base.py
  - the highest band is occupied (too few bands): increase nbnd by the larger of 5 % and 4 bands (the numbers are the values implemented in aiida-quantumespresso: delta_factor_nbnd = 0.05, delta_minimum_nbnd = 4). Source: https://github.com/aiidateam/aiida-quantumespresso/blob/main/src/aiida_quantumespresso/workflows/pw/base.py
  - 'DUE TO TIME LIMIT' (Slurm) / '=>> PBS: job killed: walltime … exceeded limit …' (PBS): the walltime limit was hit. Sources: Slurm slurmstepd https://github.com/SchedMD/slurm/blob/master/src/slurmd/slurmstepd/req.c, OpenPBS pbs_mom https://github.com/openpbs/openpbs/blob/master/src/resmom/mom_main.c
  - 'oom_kill event … OOM Killed' (Slurm) / '=>> PBS: job killed: mem …' (PBS) / 'Out of memory: Killed process' (Linux) / a 'Killed' line: killed for lack of memory. Sources: Slurm https://github.com/SchedMD/slurm/blob/master/src/plugins/task/cgroup/task_cgroup_memory.c, Linux https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/master/mm/oom_kill.c
  - 'MPI_ABORT was invoked on rank …' (Open MPI) / 'application called MPI_Abort', 'BAD TERMINATION OF ONE OF YOUR APPLICATION PROCESSES' (MPICH): an MPI process aborted; the reason is in the code's lines before it. Sources: Open MPI help-mpi-api.txt (shipped with Open MPI), MPICH https://github.com/pmodels/mpich/blob/main/src/pm/hydra/mpiexec/pmiserv_cb.c
